# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Open-source Apple Silicon research factory with a macOS/CLI runtime, an Astro
public research site, local training and evaluation workflows, and browser
experiments for smaller models.

## Users

PostTrainLLM serves learners and practitioners who want to understand ML, AI,
and language-model systems by building them on hardware they control. They need
a guided progression from first principles to models, adapters, benchmarks,
agents, packages, runtimes, and interpretability artifacts—plus repeatable
commands, explicit baselines, measurable gates, and honest decisions.

## Product Purpose

PostTrainLLM turns learning into a sequence of inspectable local artifacts, then
turns a bounded specialist objective into an evidence loop: dataset, training
run, evaluation, regression checks, package, and explicit ship, block, or park
decision.

## Positioning

It is a Mac-local LLM specialist factory and open research lab. It is not a
hosted training platform, a claim that every model can be trained on one Mac,
or a leaderboard that hides evaluation and hardware constraints.

## Operating Context

The public site explains the factory, publishes artifacts and report cards,
and exposes browser-readable and agent-readable versions of every public page.
Private training data, unpublished weights, credentials, and local run output
remain outside the public surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- MLX LoRA and QLoRA workflows target Apple Silicon.
- Evaluation uses frozen baselines, task-specific gates, and regression slices.
- Public artifacts name their evidence, hardware, limitations, and blockers.
- Browser experiments demonstrate bounded mechanisms; they do not replace
  repeatable native evaluation.
- Every learning stage ends in an artifact with explicit build, modify, tune,
  prove, and package actions.
- Runnable labs, guided evidence replays, and budgeted recipe contracts are
  labeled separately.
- A model is not release-ready without a terminal decision and retained proof.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is posttrainllm. Language is technical, concise, and candid
about what ran, what passed, what remains unverified, and what is only a recipe
or research direction. It does not fabricate model quality, hardware results,
user outcomes, or competitive superiority.

## Evidence on Hand

Public artifacts, report cards, the attempt ledger, benchmark outputs, source
code, and generated machine-readable surfaces are the available evidence.
Claims outside those sources remain clearly framed as guidance or future work.

## Product Principles

1. A frozen baseline comes before training.
2. Task outcomes and regressions outrank training loss.
3. Every artifact carries provenance, environment, and limitations.
4. Local, bounded, reproducible workflows are the default.
5. Ship, block, and park are explicit decisions—not optimistic prose.
6. Concepts should lead to things a learner can build, change, measure, and
   carry forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public research pages must be keyboard reachable, readable at narrow and wide
viewports, understandable without color alone, and available as substantive
Markdown for non-visual and agent consumers.
