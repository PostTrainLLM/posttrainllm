What mattered

Failed attempts remain first-class evidence. Candidate selection can outperform unconstrained generation on sparse tasks. Batch-first post-training, policy lag, trace review, and LoRA geometry are useful only when tied to a frozen evaluation.

The transferable idea was not “automate research.” It was to make every search step inspectable, steerable, and accountable to a retained score.

What we changed locally

PostTrainLLM added a technique registry, candidate-selection scaffolding, slice metrics, trace review, batch-plan rendering, and LoRA geometry evidence. The implementation stays bounded by one Mac and preserves the readable reference paths.

Evidence limits

We did not reproduce TrainLoop's distributed system or claim equivalent research throughput. The local translation is a method adoption, not a product comparison.

STUDY-0014 · call-only model

Needle 2

A compact tool-calling model that combines retrieval, constrained decoding, confidence escalation, bounded context, and quantization in a roughly 14 MB Mac artifact.

Readout

Reviewed → rejected for expansionThe local smoke reached 34.0% tool exactness. Catalog restriction improved the aggregate to 38.3% but regressed the Pace slice to 6/28.

The result maps a capability boundary. Catalog restriction is a latency lever, not a capability rescue, and the model should not advance to mobile actions, integration, or fine-tuning under this recipe.

Local translation

Retain the confidence-escalation contract, bounded context, and package discipline. Do not treat top-five retrieval as sufficient evidence of action grounding.

What not to copy

Do not infer general tool competence from package size, a narrow call-only benchmark, or schema-constrained decoding. The Pace regression is the decisive local counterexample.
