How to cook an LLM

A model-building recipe in three inspectable steps. Prepare inputs, compare how training can change behavior, and collect feedback. Each cell tells you exactly what changed.

# Data preparation is not pre-training.
batch = ["Rice and beans make a simple dinner.",
         "Rice and beans make a simple dinner."]
prepared = deduplicate(batch)
tokens = utf8_bytes(prepared[0])
