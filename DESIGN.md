# Design System: posttrainllm

## Overview

**Creative north star: The Local Research Bench.**

The site should feel like a working research instrument rather than a generic
AI landing page: deep ink surfaces, tightly controlled typography, explicit
evidence, and a small number of signals that carry stable meaning.

## Color

- Teal means live, current, verified, or the next primary action.
- Coral is a sparse counter-accent for warnings or deliberate contrast.
- Neutral ink layers establish hierarchy without decorative gradients.
- Semantic meaning is always written in text and never depends on color alone.

## Typography

Bricolage Grotesque gives research headings a recognizable voice. Geist keeps
long explanations calm and readable. Geist Mono is reserved for commands,
metrics, paths, states, and evidence identities.

## Layout

Use a centered workbench with generous outer gutters and readable prose capped
near 66–72 characters. Major evidence sections are divided by hairlines and
space. Dense grids become one column before content is compressed.

## Components

- Sticky top navigation stays quiet and keeps the research surface primary.
- Primary actions use live teal; secondary links remain textual or outlined.
- Cards use ink layers, a one-pixel rule, 14px corners, and restrained depth.
- Tables are factual instruments: aligned, scrollable on narrow screens, and
  labeled clearly without decorative chart chrome.
- Long-form pages begin with a small evidence label, a direct headline, a
  scoped description, and substantive sections.

## Motion

Motion may clarify state or continuity but must never gate content. All motion
stops under reduced-motion preferences.

## Do

- Show real-shaped recipes, commands, artifacts, evaluations, and blockers.
- Separate published evidence from guidance and unverified work.
- Keep agent-readable Markdown equivalent to the visible public page.

## Do not

- Add generic AI imagery, glossy gradients, or dashboard decoration.
- Invent benchmark outcomes, hardware speed, model quality, or user proof.
- Use teal as ambient decoration or coral as an unrelated second brand voice.
