## Why

PostTrainLLM has strong Apple Silicon fine-tuning and evaluation guidance but no
direct pages for the Unsloth and Axolotl comparisons that dominate model answers.
Without them, people and retrieval systems cannot easily distinguish a Mac-first
specialist factory from GPU-oriented training frameworks.

## What Changes

- Add canonical PostTrainLLM-versus-Unsloth and PostTrainLLM-versus-Axolotl pages.
- Base platform-support statements on dated official documentation and explain
  where each product is the honest fit.
- State explicitly that no controlled speed, memory, or quality comparison has
  been run.
- Reuse the existing editorial registry, sitemap, structured data, and internal
  linking system.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `apple-silicon-training-comparisons`: Public comparison pages explain hardware,
  workflow, and evaluation boundaries using primary sources and honest limitations.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The change touches only the Astro browser site, its typed education registry,
adjacent internal links, tests, and durable project status. It changes no training
runtime, model artifact, dependency, experiment, deployment, or published benchmark.
